Main Touchpoints
Three primary pages that power the event experience
🌐 Landing Page Public
https://upcycle-collective.vercel.app/
The public-facing website. Introduces the event concept, explains how the TUC token works, and guides new visitors toward the wallet. Dark "Verdant Depth" design with scroll reveal animations. Supported by Superteam Malaysia badge in the nav.
Dark Verdant Depth design What is TUC section How it works — step by step Wallet explainer + CSS phone mockup Built on Solana — stats + explainer Access — links to App & Staff Terminal Scroll reveal animations Supported by Superteam Malaysia (nav)
👛 Attendee Wallet App Attendees
https://upcycle-collective.vercel.app/app.html
Each attendee's personal wallet during the event. Requires no crypto knowledge — login with email only. Attendees use this to check their TUC balance and pay at vendor booths by scanning vendor QR codes.
Email login — no crypto wallet needed TUC balance display Scan vendor QR to pay Transaction history Zero gas fees for attendees
🛂 Staff Terminal Staff Only
https://upcycle-collective.vercel.app/staff.html
Used exclusively by event staff at the door and reward stations. Staff scan an attendee's wallet QR code to check them in and award TUC tokens for participation activities throughout the event.
QR scanner (camera) Wallet address indicator Check-in: +10 TUC Recycle Unit: +10 TUC Draft 1: +5 TUC Draft 2: +20 TUC Solscan transaction link

📊 Tokenomics Organiser
https://upcycle-collective.vercel.app/tokenomics.html
A live on-chain tracking dashboard for the organiser. Pulls data directly from Solana mainnet to show who holds TUC tokens, how many have been minted in total, and the distribution across the top holders. All data is verifiable on Solscan.
Total TUC minted Top holder % of supply Top 10 holders ranked Full wallet address per holder % share of supply Wallet → Solscan link Token Account → Solscan link Mint address with Solscan link Manual refresh
