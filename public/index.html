How it works

Simple for everyone.
Powerful underneath.

1

Log in with your email

Open the TUC wallet on your phone and enter your email. A secure Solana wallet is created for you automatically — no app download, no crypto experience needed.

Takes about 30 seconds
2

Check in at the door

Scan the check-in QR code at the entrance with your Solana wallet. TUC tokens are instantly minted to your wallet on the Solana blockchain.

TUC tokens on arrival
3

Donate bags, earn more tokens

Bring pre-loved clothing or items in a standard recycle bag. Staff scan your QR at the donation point and TUC tokens are added instantly. Every transaction is labelled on-chain.

TUC tokens per recycle bag
4

Spend at stations

Show your wallet QR at any station. Staff process the transaction and your balance updates in real time. All powered by Solana — no middleman, no delays.

QR code at every station
5

Your history lives on-chain forever

Every TUC transaction is permanently recorded on Solana with a readable memo. View your full history on Solscan anytime — even years after the event.

Fully transparent · Verifiable forever
Your wallet

What exactly
is a wallet?

Think of it like a digital bank account — but one that only you control, with no bank in between.

🔑

Your personal address on Solana

Your wallet has a unique address — like an account number. Anyone can send TUC to it, but only you can spend from it.

📱

No app to download

Works directly in your phone's browser. Log in with email, wallet is ready. Secured by Privy — a globally trusted wallet infrastructure.

👁️

Everything is visible

Every token you earn or spend is on-chain. View your full history — what you earned, what you spent, when — in the app or on Solscan.

⚡

You pay zero fees

All Solana transaction fees are paid by the event organiser. You just earn and spend — no SOL needed in your wallet, ever.

Why Solana?

Fees for attendees

Blockchain means every TUC transaction is recorded on a public, permanent ledger — not a spreadsheet that can be edited or lost. Solana is chosen because it is fast and practically free to use.

When staff scan your QR and award tokens, that transaction confirms on Solana in under a second, costs fractions of a cent, and will be readable on Solscan forever. This is not a promise — it is simply how blockchain works.

For the organiser: a fully auditable record of every token awarded and spent. For attendees: a transparent system where no one can change the numbers after the fact.
