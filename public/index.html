What is TUC

Your generosity,
turned into currency

TUC (The Upcycle Collective Token) is a digital token you earn and spend at our event. Think of it like event credits — but recorded permanently on the blockchain.

🪙

A digital token

TUC is a real token on the Solana blockchain. Every token you earn or spend is recorded permanently — transparent and verifiable by anyone.

🛍️

Earned by doing good

You earn TUC by showing up (+10 at check-in), donating recycle bags (+10 each), and completing activities at our stations throughout the day.

♻️

Spent at stations

Use your TUC tokens at activity stations — the upcycle workshop, swap corner, spice market, lucky draw, and more. No cash needed.

❤️

Backed by real action

Every token earned represents a real contribution. All donated pre-loved items go to the Salvation Army — your generosity has a real-world impact.

How it works

Simple for everyone.
Powerful underneath.

1

Log in with your email

Open the TUC wallet on your phone and enter your email. A secure Solana wallet is automatically created for you — no app download, no crypto experience needed. Your wallet lives in your browser.

Takes about 30 seconds
2

Check in at the door

Show your wallet QR code to staff at the entrance. They scan it and 10 TUC tokens are instantly minted and sent to your wallet. This happens on the Solana blockchain in under a second.

+10 TUC on arrival
3

Earn more tokens

Donate a recycle bag at the donation point to earn +10 TUC per bag. Staff scan your QR code and tokens are added instantly. Every transaction is recorded on-chain with a memo label so you can see exactly what it was for.

+10 TUC per recycle bag donated
4

Spend at stations

Use your TUC balance at any activity station around the event. Show your wallet QR, staff processes the transaction, and your balance updates in real time. All powered by Solana — no middleman, no delays.

QR code at every station
5

Your transactions live forever on-chain

Every TUC transaction — earning, spending, rewards — is permanently recorded on the Solana blockchain with a readable memo. You can view your full history on Solscan anytime, even years after the event.

Fully transparent · Verifiable forever
Your wallet

What exactly
is a wallet?

Think of it like a digital bank account — but one that only you control, with no bank in between.

🔑

It's your personal address

Your wallet has a unique address on the Solana blockchain — like an account number. Anyone can send TUC to it, but only you can spend from it.

📱

No app to download

Your TUC wallet works directly in your phone's browser. Log in with email, and your wallet is ready. Everything is secured by Privy — a trusted wallet infrastructure used globally.

👁️

Everything is visible

Every token you receive or spend is recorded on-chain. You can view your full transaction history — what you earned, what you spent, and when — directly in the app or on Solscan.

⚡

You pay zero fees

All transaction fees on Solana are paid by the event organiser. You just earn and spend — no SOL required in your wallet, ever.

Fees for attendees

Blockchain means every TUC transaction is recorded on a public, permanent ledger — not in a spreadsheet that can be changed or lost. It's the same technology behind Bitcoin and Ethereum, but Solana is chosen for one simple reason: it's fast and practically free to use.

When staff scan your QR code and award tokens, that transaction is confirmed on the Solana network in under a second, costs fractions of a cent, and will be readable on Solscan forever. This isn't a promise — it's just how the blockchain works.

For the event organiser, this means a fully auditable record of every token awarded and spent. For attendees, it means a transparent, trustworthy system where no one can change the numbers after the fact.
